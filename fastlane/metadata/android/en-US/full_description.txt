Vibra Music is an ad-free, privacy-focused audio player designed for seamless streaming and local playback. Engineered with a liquid glass aesthetic and powered by Jetpack Compose, Vibra Music provides an immersive listening experience without accounts, subscriptions, or telemetry.

Zero-Tracking Privacy
Vibra Music does not maintain any user database, does not collect analytics or crash telemetry, and never tracks your listening habits. Everything stays strictly on your device.

Playback and Audio Engine
* Stream audio from online sources with ad-free playback.
* Hi-Res lossless audio playback with support for custom module sources.
* True gapless playback with configurable crossfade (0 to 12 seconds).
* High-fidelity audio DSP: 10-band equalizer, bass boost, virtualizer, and loudness enhancer.
* Background playback with full Android MediaSession controls and Android Auto integration.
* Automix with beat-matching and tempo-stretching.

Lyrics and Visuals
* Real-time synchronized lyrics with word and syllable highlighting.
* Multi-source lyric fallback, lyric search, translation, and romanization.
* Shareable lyric poster generator with customizable backgrounds and themes.
* Dynamic liquid glass interface with colors extracted from album artwork.
* Animated album canvas artwork.

Library and Podcasts
* Discover, browse, and stream podcast shows and episodes.
* Download songs and episodes for offline listening with embedded metadata.
* Integrated local music library scanner for offline audio files.
* Synchronized listening parties with Listen Together.
* Discord Rich Presence and scrobbling to Last.fm and ListenBrainz.
